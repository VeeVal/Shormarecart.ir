# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ShomareCart a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, nationality, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project context without gatekeeping.
- Respect privacy. Sharing one's own destination card number with an intended
  payer is normal in Iran, but never publish another person's card number,
  recipient name, payment information, or personal information without consent.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose a PIN, banking password, CVV2, expiration date,
  OTP, account balance, or other confidential banking information.
- Using project spaces to promote fraud, financial abuse, or unsafe handling of payment data.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement should be fair, proportionate, and documented privately where possible.

To report conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If no private method is available, retain the evidence and request a private reporting channel without describing the incident publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Anyone involved in the reported incident should recuse themselves from handling it.
